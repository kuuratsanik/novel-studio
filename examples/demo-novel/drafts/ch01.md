# Chapter 1

The harbor smelled of salt and rust. [[Iskra]] watched the lantern swing on the dock chain.
She kept her boots on the dry planks and counted the gaps between the boards the way other people counted prayers.
Below, black water slapped the pilings. A ferry horn cut the fog once, then again, farther out.

Iskra checked the sealed packet under her coat. The wax still held. Deliver before first light, or do not come back.
She stepped past a sleeping watchman and into the alley that led toward the warehouse lights.
